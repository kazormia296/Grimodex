# C2-ZC Canonical Freshness Cutover Impact Matrix

This matrix is the boundary design record for C2-ZC.  The feature remains a
main/N-API/shared-Rust maintenance seam; renderer and preload do not gain a
cutover or scheduler-maintenance surface.

## Authority decision

The durable authority marker is owned by the schema migration module, while
the C2-ZC decision is owned by shared Rust.  The live scheduler proof is not a
free-form timestamp: the N-API call obtains the current main-process authority
binding and shared Rust mints/registers the receipt.  Cutover accepts that
receipt only while its exact authority/generation and monotonic age remain
current.  This keeps the marker durable without making it a second Freshness
authority and keeps restart/swap recovery fail closed.
